Platform

Approval engine, outcome model

Steps that hand structured data back to the module

The engine owns whether something was approved. The module owns what stage it is in. Holding that line let five other modules keep their integrations untouched while the engine grew.

AI add-on

Natural-language record creation

Describe what you need, review the draft, submit it yourself

The assistant proposes a filled form and never writes to the database. Piloted on the budget flow because a wrong suggestion there costs ten seconds, not a mis-posted financial record.

HR

Blue-collar timesheet

Legacy and new data under one approval model

Supervisors approve a day rather than a scatter of rows, over a view that unifies two data models so the seam is invisible to the person doing the work.

Project docs

Transmittal management

Migration in deltas, print templates, follow-up, write-back

The part that changed people's days was the follow-up screen. Before it existed, "where is this document" was a phone call.
